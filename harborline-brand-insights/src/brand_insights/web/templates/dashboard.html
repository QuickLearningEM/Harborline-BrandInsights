What do these terms mean?

Responsiveness compares a segment's return per ad dollar with the average across all segments. 100 is average; 150 means 50% better.

Under-invested: responds well but gets less budget than its share of revenue. Over-invested: responds poorly but gets more budget than its share of revenue. Message mismatch: spend looks reasonable, but public comments are negative on price, quality, or trust. Aligned: no change suggested.
